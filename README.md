## 👩‍💻 About me

I hold an **MSc in Computer Engineering — Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Robotics** from the **University of Salerno**.

My interests include **machine learning, computer vision, robotics and biomedical signal analysis**. I mainly work with **Python**, and I enjoy turning a complex problem into an experiment I can implement, evaluate and improve.

For my Master's thesis, I worked on **epileptic seizure detection from EEG signals**, including an **Erasmus+ research stay at INSA Rouen Normandie, LITIS laboratory** (February–May 2026).

I am looking for opportunities to contribute to AI projects and grow alongside experienced engineers.

## 🧬 Master's thesis — EEG seizure detection

<details open>
<summary><b>Development of a monitoring system for patients with epilepsy based on EEG signal processing</b></summary>

Research on deep learning approaches for epileptic seizure detection using the **CHB-MIT dataset**. The work covered EEG preprocessing, windowing, implementation and comparison of neural network architectures, and evaluation with sensitivity, specificity and balanced accuracy.

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Research setting** | University of Salerno · Erasmus+ at INSA Rouen Normandie / LITIS |
| **Focus** | EEG signal analysis · seizure detection · deep learning |
| **Approach** | Data preprocessing · model training · comparative evaluation |
| **Code** | [TesiAsja](https://github.com/AsjaAntonucci/TesiAsja) |

</details>
